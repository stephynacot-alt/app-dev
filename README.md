# My Favorite Movie: Inception (2010)

> "An idea is like a virus. Resilient. Highly contagious. And even the smallest seed of an idea can grow." — *Dom Cobb*

---

## Overview
**Inception** is a sci-fi action thriller written and directed by **Christopher Nolan**. The story follows a professional thief who steals secrets by infiltrating the subconscious minds of his targets.

### Key Details
- **Release Year:** 2010
- **Director:** Christopher Nolan
- **Genre:** Sci-Fi / Action / Mystery
- **Starring:** 
  - Leonardo DiCaprio as *Dom Cobb*
  - Joseph Gordon-Levitt as *Arthur*
  - Elliot Page as *Ariadne*
  - Tom Hardy as *Eames*

---

## Top 3 Favorite Themes
1. **Dream Within a Dream:** Exploring multi-layered subconscious levels.
2. **The Totem:** A physical object used to determine whether a person is in reality or a dream.
3. **The Score:** The iconic soundtrack composed by *Hans Zimmer*.
